# Aligning Large Language Models to the public: A background study and project proposal

This repository contains my "research essay" for the [AIML501 course](https://www.wgtn.ac.nz/courses/aiml/501/2025) at [Victoria University of Wellington](https://www.wgtn.ac.nz/). This essay is effectively a literature review (with a research statement and research proposal added) on the alignment of large language models (LLMs).

It was completed in Trimester 3 2025 (Nov 2025-Feb 2026) under the supervision of [Dr. Alistair Knott](https://people.wgtn.ac.nz/ali.knott). It was about 150 hours of work.

There was a strict word limit of 10,000 words (excluding references and appendices).
